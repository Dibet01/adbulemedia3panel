Frequently asked questions

Is Vaultra free to use? +
Yes. Browsing and starting an offer is free. Each offer is run by a third-party partner, and their own terms apply once you continue to their site.
Who fulfills these offers? +
Vaultra doesn't fulfill offers directly. Each card above links to an advertising partner (e.g. CTConnect, SuperSweepstakes, PrizeZappy) who runs and fulfills that specific offer.
Why do offers change? +
Our partners rotate availability based on demand and region, so the list updates regularly. If an offer is unavailable, check back soon for a new one.
Do I need to make a purchase? +
No purchase is required to view or start an offer. Some partner offers may involve optional steps like sign-ups — always read the partner's terms before continuing.
